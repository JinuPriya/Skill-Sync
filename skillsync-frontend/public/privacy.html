Privacy Policy

Skill Sync · Last updated: November 26, 2026

Skill Sync ("we", "our", "the app") is a web application that helps users connect and schedule skill-sharing sessions. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. The app is available at https://skill-sync-five-sandy.vercel.app/.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and a password (stored only in hashed form) when you register.
  • Session information: details of the skill sessions you create, join, or schedule, such as title, date, time, and participants.
  • Google account data: if you authorize Google, we access your Google Calendar only to create calendar events with Google Meet links for the sessions you schedule.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you log in.
  • To create, display, and manage skill sessions.
  • To generate Google Meet links and calendar events for your sessions.
  • To send you emails about your sessions, such as invitations and confirmations.

3. Google user data

Skill Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the calendar and Google Meet features described above. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties, except as required by law.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Basic session details (such as your name and the meeting link) are shared with the other participants of a session you create or join. We use third-party services to run the app (hosting, database, and email delivery), which process data only on our behalf.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in a cloud database. Passwords are hashed, and we take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. You can also revoke Skill Sync's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Children's privacy

Skill Sync is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
